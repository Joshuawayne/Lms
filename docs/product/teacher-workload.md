# Teacher workload requirements

- Record once and reuse appropriate evidence in portfolios and reports.
- Provide reusable activities, rubrics, feedback templates and contextual defaults.
- Support quick session recording and deeper assessment at suitable milestones.
- Rotate focused observations fairly across learners instead of requiring all
  values for every learner in every session.
- Reuse group evidence while retaining individual contributions and judgments.
- Let learners contribute evidence where age, access and permissions allow.
- Assemble report drafts from existing records, with teacher review before release.
- Keep enrolment, account administration, imports and scheduling with administrators.
- Offer manageable notifications, explainable suggestions and teacher overrides.
- Keep teacher reflections private under the agreed visibility rules.

Measure success through useful learning evidence and reduced administrative work,
with teacher feedback on whether the workflow actually saves time.
